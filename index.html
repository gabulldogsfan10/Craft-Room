How the room works

  • Four zones, one per wall. Machine embroidery on the long blank east wall, hand embroidery in the daylight of the window seat, beading/jewelry/art supplies on the west wall, bulk storage in the closet.
  • Centre stays flexible. The NORDEN gateleg table folds to 10¼″, so the floor clears for laying out fabric or opening the closet fully; opened to 59⅞″ it is a cutting and beading table.
  • Door swings are kept clear. The east counter stops before the entry door, and nothing sits in the arc of the closet doors (see the floor plan).

Machine embroidery

  • The KARLBY counter is about 25″ deep: room for the embroidery unit and hoop to travel without hitting the wall. Keep about 6″ clear behind and to the left of the machine.
  • Counter height lands at about 29″ – comfortable sewing height. Adjust the LÅNGFJÄLL chair so your forearms are level with the machine bed.
  • Hoops hang on the SKÅDIS pegboards; thread cones line the LACK shelf; the ALEX drawers hold stabilizer, bobbins, needles and design USB sticks.
  • Plan for outlets: there is one on this wall today. Use a surge-protected power strip mounted under the counter.

Hand embroidery & the window seat

  • Two 47¼″ BESTÅ frames on the floor (no legs) make a 94½″ seat centred on the 69½″ window, about 12½″ wider on each side; with the cushion the seat is about 18″ high – standard chair height.
  • Four deep drawers hold hoops, floss boxes and works-in-progress. Push-open runners keep the fronts handle-free.
  • North light is even and shadow-free – ideal for stitching. The HEKTAR floor lamp covers evenings; the RÅSKOG cart rolls over with your floss.
  • Anchor the BESTÅ frames to the wall – they will be stood and knelt on.

Beading, jewelry & art

  • The two ALEX 9-drawer units have shallow drawers – the best format for beads and findings. KUGGIS lidded boxes keep colours separate and can be carried to the table.
  • The KALLAX 4×4 mixes closed storage (drawers/doors for paint and markers), DRÖNA bins (yarn, felt, scraps) and open cubes (pads, books, pattern files).
  • Work on a light surface: lay a light cutting mat or bead board on the NORDEN when beading so dropped beads are easy to spot.

Before you buy

  • The window width (69½″) is measured. Still measure the sill height and the closet interior – the drawing assumes a 27½″ sill and an 8′ ceiling.
  • Confirm every IKEA size on ikea.com; product lines are refreshed often.
  • Secure all tall pieces (KALLAX, ALEX 9-drawer) to the wall with the included anti-tip hardware.

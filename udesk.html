Not from IKEA

IKEA doesn't sell the joining hardware: a wood cleat screwed along the wall under the back edges, mending plates or countertop connector bolts for the seams, sealer (KARLBY) or iron-on edge banding (laminate) for cut edges, and – for Option 3 – 2¾″ blocks (or furniture risers) under the NORDEN's gateleg feet. IKEA doesn't cut countertops either – use a circular saw with a straight guide, or a hardware store that cuts. Prices are IKEA US list prices; check them when you order.
